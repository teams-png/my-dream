✨What you can do

Send a professional quotation, turn it into a sales order when the customer agrees, deliver in one or more trips with a delivery note, and invoice what was delivered — without typing anything twice.

QuotationItems, prices, validity date and notes/terms. Print, PDF or WhatsApp.
ConvertOne button turns a quotation into a sales order or straight into an invoice.
Partial deliveryDeliver some now and the rest later; each trip gets its own delivery note.
Price lists & offersSpecial prices for a customer or a period are filled in automatically.

These tools are in the Sales group of the left menu, the same for every business type.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Check your products and customers
  1. Products and customers must exist first — but you can also type a New customer name right on the quotation.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Write a quotation
  1. Sales → Quotations → New quotation. Choose the customer, Add item for each line, set Valid until and Notes / terms. Save.
  2. Press Send on WhatsApp or Print / PDF, then Mark as sent.
Customer said yes
  1. Open the quotation → Customer accepted → Convert to sales order (or Convert to invoice for a simple job).
Deliver and invoice
  1. Open the sales order → Deliver now. Enter the quantity for this trip.
  2. Choose Deliver & invoice to bill at once, or Deliver only and later Invoice delivered items.
  3. Print the Delivery note for the driver; the customer signs Received by.
Good to know: Stock goes down when goods are delivered, not when the order is taken.
Set special prices
  1. Price lists & offers → Create price list for a customer, or Save offer for a period (e.g. Ramadan 10%). The POS and new quotations use them automatically.
Track leads
  1. Leads & pipeline → New lead. Move it through the pipeline, add follow-ups, and when they buy press Convert to customer.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Converting never re-types items or prices.
  • Expired quotations are marked Expired.
  • Invoices post to your accounts and appear in Money to collect.
