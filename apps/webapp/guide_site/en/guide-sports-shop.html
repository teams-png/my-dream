Find your section

After you sign in, look at the left menu. Below the common groups you will see a section for your business: Sports Shop. Everything in this guide starts from the items inside it.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add units, brands and categories
  1. Open Units → add pcs, pair, set.
  2. Open Brands → add brands such as Nike, Adidas, SG, Yonex.
  3. Open Categories → add shop sections such as Footwear, Apparel, Equipment.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Add a sports product
  1. Open Products → + Add product.
  2. Fill SKU, Name, Category, Unit, Cost price and Selling price.
  3. Choose Sport category, type the Size, choose Gender (Men, Women, Unisex, Kids) and Material.
  4. Save. You will see “Product added.”
Tip: Same shoe in many sizes? Add one product per size (e.g. NIKE-AIR-UK8) so stock per size is correct.
Receive stock from a supplier
  1. Open Purchases → Purchases → + New purchase and pick the supplier, product, quantity and unit cost.
  2. Stock increases straight away.
Good to know: More on suppliers: Getting started → Suppliers & purchases.
Sell at the counter
  1. Open Sales → POS, search by name or SKU (or use the barcode), set quantity, choose payment method and press Complete Sale.
Print barcode labels
  1. Open Print Barcodes, choose products and number of labels, then Generate labels.
Handle a return or exchange
  1. Open Returns & Refunds, enter the invoice number, select what is returned and choose how to refund.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Every POS sale creates an invoice, reduces stock and updates your books.
  • Low stock alerts appear on the dashboard and in Notifications.
  • Analytics show sales by day, payment methods and top-selling products.
