Find your section

After you sign in, look at the left menu. Below the common groups you will see a section for your business: Beauty Parlour. Everything in this guide starts from the items inside it.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add your services
  1. Open Beauty Parlour → Services → + Add service.
  2. Enter Name, Duration (minutes) and Price.
  3. Repeat for everything you offer, for example: Facial, Threading, Bridal makeup, Manicure.
Tip: Keep names short and clear — they appear on invoices.
Add your staff (beauticians)
  1. Open Staff → + Add staff.
  2. Enter Name, Phone, Role title (e.g. Beautician), Salary and Joined date.
  3. Save. They now appear in the booking form.
Good to know: Staff here means people who do the work. Logins for your team are different — see Team & roles in Getting started.
Add your customers
  1. Open People → Customers → + Add customer with name and phone. You can also add them while you book.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Book an appointment
  1. Open Appointments → + Book appointment.
  2. Choose the Customer, the Service, the Beautician and the date & time.
  3. Enter the Beautician commission % if you pay commission (leave 0 if not).
  4. Save. You will see the booking with status Booked.
Complete an appointment after the service
  1. Open Appointments and find the booking.
  2. Press Complete. The status changes to Completed and the staff commission (if any) is recorded.
Cancel or mark a no-show
  1. Open Appointments → press Cancel on the booking. It is counted under Cancelled/No-show in your reports.
Change a price or add a service later
  1. Open Services → press Edit to change name, duration or price; or + Add service for a new one.
  2. Old appointments keep the price they were booked with.
Careful: A service that already has appointments cannot be deleted — edit it instead.
See your day at a glance
  1. The Overview page shows Appointments today and completed totals.
  2. Open Reports to see Booked / Completed / Cancelled counts, revenue and revenue by service.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Booking creates the invoice for the service price automatically.
  • Completing the appointment records the staff commission you entered.
  • Reports summarise bookings and revenue per service without any extra work.

❓Common questions

I booked at the wrong time.

Cancel the booking and book a new one at the right time.

Where do I see staff commission?

It is recorded against the staff member when you press Complete. Your accountant can view it in the accounts.

The staff drop-down is empty.

Add staff first under Staff → + Add staff.
