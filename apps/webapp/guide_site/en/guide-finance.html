These tools are in the Finance group of the left menu, the same for every business type.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add bank and cash accounts
  1. Finance → Bank & cash → Add account with the opening balance.
Tax & currency
  1. Settings → Tax & currency: tax registration number, tax codes and exchange rates.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Record an expense
  1. Expenses → + Add expense: category, amount, date, paid from cash or bank.
Reconcile the bank
  1. Bank & cash → Import bank statement (CSV from your bank) → Match each line, or Record & match what is missing.
Manage a post-dated cheque
  1. Cheques (PDC) → Add a cheque. Mark it Deposited, Cleared ✓ or Bounced when it happens.
Set up a recurring invoice
  1. Recurring invoices → New schedule: customer, items, repeat (monthly…), first date. It is created automatically every day it is due.
Depreciate assets
  1. Fixed assets → Add asset, then each month Post depreciation. Use Sell / scrap when it leaves.
Set budgets
  1. Budgets → a monthly budget per expense category and a Warn at %. You are alerted when spending crosses it.
Get the daily report
  1. Daily report → tick Email me this report every night, add more emails, or Send on WhatsApp.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Double-entry bookkeeping happens in the background.
  • Recurring invoices and depreciation run on schedule.
  • Over-budget and cheque-due reminders appear in Notifications.
