Find your section

These tools are in the Settings group of the left menu, the same for every business type.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Choose your language
  1. Press the 🌐 button at the top-right and pick your language. Each person can choose their own.
Switch on two-step login
  1. Settings → Security & 2FA → install Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy → scan the QR code → type the 6-digit code.
  2. Save the recovery codes somewhere safe.
Careful: Strongly recommended for owners and admins.
Check your backups
  1. Backups: automatic daily backup is on. Press Back up now any time, or Connect Google Drive for an extra copy.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Give someone a login
  1. People → Team → add a person, pick a role and (optional) the branches they work at.
See who changed what
  1. Activity log lists every important change with the user and time.
Choose reminders
  1. Reminders & alerts: customer bills due, supplier bills due, stock expiring, memberships ending, low stock — and how many days before.
Download all your data
  1. Settings → Backup & export → Download all my data (zip).

⚙️What the system does for you

  • A backup is made every night.
  • Reminders are checked every morning.
  • Sign-in is locked for a while after many wrong passwords.

❓Common questions

I lost my phone with the authenticator.

Sign in with one of your recovery codes, then set up two-step login again.

Does changing language change my data?

No. Only the screens change; your products and customers stay as typed.

Stuck? We are one message away.
