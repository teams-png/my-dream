Find your section

After you sign in, look at the left menu. Below the common groups you will see a section for your business: Cycle Shop. Everything in this guide starts from the items inside it.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add brands, categories and products
  1. Open Brands (e.g. Hero, Hercules, Giant), Categories (Cycles, Spares, Accessories) and Units (pcs).
  2. Open Products → + Add product for each cycle model and spare part.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Add a cycle unit (each physical cycle)
  1. Open Cycle Units → + Add unit.
  2. Choose the Product (model), type the Serial number, Warranty (months) and Purchase price. Keep status In Stock.
Sell a cycle
  1. On Cycle Units press Sell next to the cycle.
  2. Choose the Customer, Sale price and Sold date. You will see “Sold … (#serial)”.
Good to know: Only In Stock cycles can be sold.
Open a service ticket
  1. Open Service Tickets → + New ticket.
  2. Choose the Customer. If the cycle was bought from you pick it in Cycle; otherwise type a Cycle description.
  3. Describe the Issue, optionally choose Staff, and set Received date. Save.
Move the ticket along
  1. Press Start when work begins (status In progress).
  2. Press Complete when the repair is done.
  3. Press Deliver when the customer collects the cycle.
Good to know: Tickets track the status of each job. Charge for parts and labour by adding a service item as a product and selling it through the POS.
Sell spares and accessories
  1. Use Sales → POS — search the item, add it to the cart and complete the sale.
Check your numbers
  1. Open Reports to see cycles In stock, Sold, Revenue and Open tickets.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Selling a cycle creates an invoice and marks the unit as Sold.
  • Spare-part sales reduce stock and post to your books automatically.
  • The dashboard shows how many tickets are still open.

❓Common questions

Can I edit a cycle’s details?

Yes — press Edit on the Cycle Units page. A unit with service tickets cannot be deleted.

Can I open a ticket for a cycle I did not sell?

Yes. Leave the cycle empty and type a description like “Blue Hero 26-inch”.
