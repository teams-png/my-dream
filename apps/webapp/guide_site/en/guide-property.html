Find your section

After you sign in, look at the left menu. Below the common groups you will see a section for your business: Properties. Everything in this guide starts from the items inside it.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add your properties and units
  1. Open Properties → Properties & units → add the building, then add each unit with its normal rent.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Create a lease
  1. Open Leases → New lease. Choose the unit, choose or type the tenant, enter start/end dates, monthly rent and deposit. Save.
Bill this month's rent
  1. Open Monthly rent → choose the month → Create the rent invoices. Leases already billed are skipped.
End a lease
  1. Open the lease and close it. The unit becomes vacant.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Rent invoices post to your accounts.
  • A lease is never billed twice for the same month.
  • Units become vacant when a lease is closed.
