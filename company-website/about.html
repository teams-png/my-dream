Our story

Built for the businesses around us

MMM Technologie, led by Mariam Mohammed Almalek, started with a simple idea: small and growing businesses deserve the same quality of software and IT that large companies have, at a price that makes sense.

That idea became BookPilot, one app for billing, stock, staff and accounts that is ready for more than a hundred types of business. Around it we built a full IT service: websites, apps, networks and support, so our customers have one partner for everything technology.
