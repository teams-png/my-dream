Features

Everything a growing business needs

Start with billing on day one and switch on more as you grow. Everything shares the same products, customers and accounts.

Fast POS & billing

Barcode scanning, receipt printers, cash drawer and card machine. Bills go out on WhatsApp or email.

Stock & purchases

Stock per branch, low-stock alerts, batches and expiry, IMEI/serials, purchase orders and supplier dues.

Accounts & VAT

Ledger, profit & loss, balance sheet, bank, cheques and VAT reports, plus ZATCA QR for Saudi invoices.

Customers & dues

Statements, money to collect, loyalty points and one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders.

Staff, HR & payroll

Attendance, salaries, advances, WPS file, document expiry alerts and roles that decide who sees what.

Reports & Today page

Sales, best sellers and profit at a glance, plus a daily owner summary on email or WhatsApp.

Website & online shop

Your products online with cart and checkout. Orders land straight in BookPilot.

Many branches

Run every shop from one account, move stock between branches and compare them.

Quick to use

Ctrl+K search, keyboard shortcuts, quick edit and one-click status so daily work takes less time.
