🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Set reorder levels
  1. On each product set Reorder at. Items at or below it show as Running low.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Correct stock (damage, loss, own use)
  1. Stock & inventory → Adjust stock. Choose the item, location, Add to stock or Remove from stock and the reason.
Do a stock count
  1. Start stock taking → Start counting (optionally one category). Scan or type each item's counted quantity; leave uncounted items empty.
  2. Save progress any time. When done press Finish & correct stock.
Receive a batch with expiry
  1. Batches & expiry → Receive a batch: item, batch/lot number, expiry date, quantity. Expired stock can be Write off.
Add serial / IMEI numbers
  1. Serial numbers → Add serial numbers to stock — one per line, or scan one after another.
Move stock between branches
  1. Transfer stock → choose From and To, items and quantities → Move stock.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Every sale, purchase, return and transfer updates stock.
  • Low-stock and expiry reminders are created every day.
