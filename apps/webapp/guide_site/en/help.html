💬What the messages mean

Red or yellow messages appear at the top of the page. They tell you exactly what to fix.

Can’t delete — … linked to it.The item is already used in sales, stock or appointments.Edit it instead. Deleting used records would break your accounts.

❓Frequently asked questions

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the website on your phone and press Install app, or install the Android / iPhone app. The POS and kitchen screens also work on tablets.

Can I use it in my language?

Yes — 12 languages including Malayalam, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Tamil and more. Press 🌐 at the top-right. Each person can choose their own language.

Can two or more people work at the same time?

Yes. Give each person their own login under People → Team so you always know who did what.

I made a mistake in a sale. Can I edit or delete the invoice?

To keep your accounts correct, invoices are not deleted. Use Returns & Refunds for the wrong sale and sell again correctly.

Is my data safe? Can other businesses see my data?

No. Each business is completely separate — you only ever see your own company’s data. Do not share your password; give each staff member their own login.

Where do I print an invoice or receipt?

After a POS sale press Print receipt. For older sales open the sales list and press PDF.

How do I get my data into Excel?

Most lists have an Excel or Export CSV button. To download everything, use Settings → Backup & export → Download all my data.

What if the internet stops?

The POS keeps working offline and syncs your bills when the connection is back.

Can I add a new branch or another business later?

Branches: Business → Branches. A separate business can be added by us — message us.

Who can see reports and money?

Only roles with permission. The Staff role cannot see financial reports by default.
