Our product

BookPilot: billing, stock, staff and accounts in one place

Made for shops, restaurants, salons, clinics and service companies. Open it on any computer, tablet or phone, sell from the counter, and see how the business is doing at a glance.

Fast POS & billing

Barcode scanning, receipt printers, cash drawer and card machine. Bills go to customers on WhatsApp or email.

Stock & purchases

Stock in every branch, low-stock alerts, batches and expiry, IMEI/serial numbers, purchase orders and supplier dues.

Accounts & VAT

Ledger, profit & loss, balance sheet, bank, cheques and VAT reports, plus ZATCA QR invoices for Saudi customers.

Customers & dues

Customer accounts, statements, money to collect, loyalty points and one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders.

Staff, HR & payroll

Attendance, salaries, advances, WPS file, document expiry alerts and roles that decide who sees what.

Reports & Today page

Sales, best sellers and profit in clear reports, plus a daily owner summary by email or WhatsApp.

Website & online shop

A website with your products, cart and online orders that land straight in BookPilot, on your own domain.

Branches

Run many shops from one account. Move stock between branches and see reports for one branch or all.

Safe & private

Each business's data is kept separate, with two-step login, an audit log of every important change and daily backups.
