Find your section

After you sign in, look at the left menu. Below the common groups you will see a section for your business: Bookings. Everything in this guide starts from the items inside it.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add what you rent out
  1. Open Bookings → your list (it is named for your business: Rooms, Vehicles, Equipment, Halls or Spaces).
  2. Press Add and enter the name or number, the rate and the rate unit (per night, day, hour or event).
Switch on online booking (optional)
  1. Open Online booking → Booking settings: switch it on and set minimum notice and how many days ahead people can book.
  2. Put the form on your website — see the Website & online orders guide.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Make a booking
  1. Open Booking calendar and tap an empty day on the right row — or press ＋ New booking.
  2. Choose the customer (or type a new name and phone), the From and Until dates/times and the number of guests.
  3. Enter the Advance paid now and the Security deposit if you take one. Press Book.
Careful: The end must be after the start. A room or vehicle that is already booked for those dates cannot be booked twice.
Check in / hand over
  1. Open the booking and press Check in / hand over. The calendar shows it as In use.
Check out and bill
  1. Press Check out & bill. Add extras (minibar, fuel, damage) and any discount.
  2. Press Check out & create invoice. Late return time is included automatically. The advance is taken off the bill.
Return the deposit
  1. When everything is fine press Deposit returned on the booking.
Change dates, cancel or mark no-show
  1. Open the booking → Change dates / move, Cancel booking or No-show.
Handle online requests
  1. Open Online booking → Booking requests → Confirm booking or Decline. Then tell the customer on WhatsApp with one tap.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Double bookings are blocked.
  • Late returns are charged at your rate.
  • The advance is deducted from the final invoice and posted to your accounts.
  • Today's arrivals, check-outs and overdue returns are shown at the top of the calendar.

The names follow your business type. The steps are the same.

Yes. Leave the deposit empty.
