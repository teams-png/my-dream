Find your section

These tools are in the People group of the left menu, the same for every business type.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Switch on online payment (optional)
  1. Finance → Online payments — connect your payment gateway so invoices get a Pay now button.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Receive a payment
  1. People → Money to collect → the customer → Receive payment. Enter the amount; it is applied to the oldest bills first. Save payment.
Send a reminder
  1. On the customer press Send reminder — a polite WhatsApp message with the amount due and pay link.
Print a statement
  1. Open the customer → Statement → Print statement, or Copy customer portal link.
Set a credit limit
  1. Customer → Credit settings: Credit limit (0 = no limit) and Days to pay.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Overdue bills appear in Reminders every morning.
  • Online payments mark the invoice paid by themselves.
