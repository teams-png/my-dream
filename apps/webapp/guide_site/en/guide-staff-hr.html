Find your section

These tools are in the People group of the left menu, the same for every business type.

The common groups (Sales, Purchases, Finance, People, Business, Settings) are the same for every business and are explained in Getting started.

🚀Day-1 setup

Do these once, in this order, before your first day of work.

Add your staff
  1. People → Staff & HR → Add staff: name, job title, basic salary, allowances and (optional) app login.
HR settings
  1. HR settings: overtime rate, leave types and holidays.

🛠️Everyday tasks

Click a heading to open or close it. Steps are numbered in the order you press.

Mark attendance
  1. Attendance → choose the day → mark each person, or Mark all present. Save.
Approve leave
  1. Leave & holidays → Add leave or approve requests in Leave to approve.
Give a salary advance
  1. Payroll & advances → Give advance: amount and monthly deduction.
Run payroll
  1. Run payroll → Calculate payroll → check the draft → Approve payroll → Pay all (cash or bank). Print payslips.

⚙️What the system does for you

  • Absence and advance recovery are deducted automatically.
  • Expiring documents appear in reminders.
  • Salaries post to your accounts.
